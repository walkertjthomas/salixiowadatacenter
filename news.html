The Salix Annexation Timeline

A comprehensive, factual record of the proposed 900-acre data center development, rezoning efforts, and community pushback.

August 20, 2026

City Council Reverses Course on Moratorium

Amid high tension and a heavy police presence to maintain order, the Salix City Council votes 3-2 against enacting a one-year moratorium on data center developments. Council Member Denise Burkhart changes her previous July vote in order to allow conversations with Google to continue. The failure of the moratorium allows the data center rezoning process to proceed.

August 12, 2026

Threats Force Meeting Postponement

A highly anticipated City Council meeting to vote on finalized moratorium language is abruptly postponed. Mayor Kevin Nelson cites death and terroristic threats made against city officials on social media. The Woodbury County Sheriff's Office launches an investigation.

August 6, 2026

Woodbury County Files Lawsuit Against Salix

Woodbury County and three area landowners officially file legal petitions challenging the city's initial annexation of the 900-acre farmland plot. The county alleges Salix failed to properly notify residents, circumvented state code, and illegally annexed property that does not actually border the city limits.

August 3, 2026

Google Identified as Potential Developer

MidAmerican Energy publicly identifies Google as the potential primary customer interested in the site. Google confirms it has reached an agreement to investigate a potential Salix development, but notes no formal rezoning or development applications have been filed yet.

July 8, 2026

Initial Moratorium Drafted

Facing growing community concerns over water, energy, and infrastructure strain, the Salix City Council votes 3-2 to draft a one-year moratorium on data center developments. The city attorney is tasked with drafting the official language for a final August vote.

June 2026

County Acts While Data Center Rumors Swirl

As reports emerge that MidAmerican Energy is evaluating the 900-acre Salix site for a massive potential data center development, the Woodbury County Board of Supervisors unanimously passes its own moratorium on data centers in unincorporated areas of the county in direct response to Salix's maneuvers.

April 8, 2026

Salix Annexes 900 Acres

Following a March notice in the Sergeant Bluff Advocate, the Salix City Council unanimously approves a voluntary annexation petition, officially bringing roughly 900 acres of agricultural farmland into the city's jurisdiction.
