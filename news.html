Following the failure of the moratorium, a community petition titled "Stop the Salix, Iowa Data Center Project" launches, demanding officials pause the project. August 20, 2026

City Council Reverses Course on Moratorium (KSCJ 1360)

Amid high tension and a heavy police presence to maintain order, the Salix City Council votes 3-2 against enacting a one-year moratorium on data center developments. Council Member Denise Burkhart changes her previous July vote in order to allow conversations with Google to continue. The failure of the moratorium allows the data center rezoning process to proceed.

https://kscj.com/2026/08/21/salix-council-votes-down-data-center-moratorium/
August 12, 2026

Threats Force Meeting Postponement (Sioux City Journal)

A highly anticipated City Council meeting to vote on finalized moratorium language is abruptly postponed. Mayor Kevin Nelson cites death and terroristic threats made against city officials on social media. The Woodbury County Sheriff's Office launches an investigation. Internal documents later reveal Google representatives had explicitly planned to attend this meeting to begin direct community negotiations.

Woodbury County Files Lawsuit Against Salix (Iowa Public Radio)

Woodbury County and three area landowners officially file legal petitions challenging the city's initial annexation of the 900-acre farmland plot. The county alleges Salix failed to properly notify residents, circumvented state code, and illegally annexed property that does not actually border the city limits.
