Community Action

The Siouxland deserves transparency. Here is how you can get involved and ensure our community's voice is heard.

Upcoming Meetings

  • Salix City Council Meeting: The City Council meets on the second Wednesday of each month at 7:00 PM. Meetings are held in the Council Chambers located directly behind City Hall at 317 Tipton St., Salix, IA 51052.
  • Woodbury County Zoning Commission: Meets on the fourth Monday of each month at 5:00 PM. Meetings take place at 620 Douglas St., Sioux City, IA 51101.
  • Woodbury County Board of Adjustment: Meets on the first Monday of the month.

Contact Your Representatives

Call or email the Salix City Council to demand guarantees on water usage, noise limits, and local infrastructure support. If you want to speak at a meeting, you must contact them to be added to the agenda.
