When: Fourth Monday of each month at 5:00 PM.
Where: 620 Douglas St., Sioux City, IA 51101.

City Master Planning Documents

Before attending a meeting, residents may wish to review the city's official long-term developmental goals. The 2035 Salix Comprehensive Plan details the foundational land-use and zoning philosophies intended to guide the community's growth over the next decade.
